Part I ─ Debugging

Learn debugging with visual puzzles and Java code.

Explore the cryptic secrets of a mysterious planet.

Aboard the Sojourner, humanity’s last hope drifts silently between the stars, sleeping unconsciously in cryogenic pods, unknowing where their journey will take them.

Then the ship’s systems detect a mysterious planet: Earlier calculations determined it as inhabitable, yet its surface is covered in structures. Whoever built them has vanished without a trace, except for a few sealed temples, containing worn down machinery.

You were tasked to explore the planet, to recover any technology that might help humanity survive, and to investigate the history of the planet.
A service robot accompanies you to the surface, aiding you with analyzing the planet and its ancient machinery. You can also use tools aboard the Sojourner for your research; though they are worn down by the long journey and may need some maintenance. By observing carefully, forming hypotheses and narrowing down the fault, you debug each system and, piece by piece, uncover what happened on this planet.
